STEP 01

Bring in the product

Pick whichever method works on your device. The one-click button is the most reliable on a computer.

★ Send to Review Sprint ← drag this to your bookmarks bar
  1. One-time setup: drag the orange button to your bookmarks bar. Press Ctrl+Shift+B (⌘+Shift+B on Mac) if the bar is hidden.
  2. Open any Amazon product page and click Send to Review Sprint in your bookmarks.
  3. This app opens with the title, features, specs, and photo already filled in.

The bookmark reads the page you're already viewing, so Amazon can't block it. It only sends product details to this page. Nothing goes to a server.

We pick out the title, feature bullets, description, and specs. Nothing is uploaded or saved.

We try a public page reader first. With Gemini turned on in AI settings, we can also look the product up with Google Search, which works far more often.
